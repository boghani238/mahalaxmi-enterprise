Privacy Policy

App: Universal TV Remote Control
Developer: Mahalaxmi Enterprise

This Privacy Policy explains how the Universal TV Remote Control app (“the App”) handles information when you use it.

Important: This page is a starter policy. Before publishing, make sure it accurately reflects the SDKs, permissions, analytics, advertising services and data practices in your released app.

1. Information and permissions

The App may use device capabilities and local network access needed to provide remote-control functions. For example, infrared functionality requires a compatible built-in IR transmitter, while Wi-Fi functionality may require the phone and supported TV to be connected to the same network. The App should request only permissions needed for its features.

2. How information is used

Information, if collected by the App or its integrated services, may be used to operate and improve app features, diagnose technical issues, maintain security, and provide advertising or analytics services where enabled.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The App may include third-party services such as Google Play services or advertising and analytics SDKs. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Please review the applicable provider policies and ensure this section matches the SDKs actually included in your app.

4. Data sharing

Mahalaxmi Enterprise does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate app functionality, comply with legal obligations, or protect users and the service.

5. Data retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information handled by the App and its service providers.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

7. Your choices

You can stop using the App or uninstall it at any time. You can also manage app permissions through your Android device settings. Some features may not work if required permissions are denied.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be made available on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through the support/contact details provided on the App’s Google Play listing.
